How it works

Vice runs in the background until you need it.

No scene setup and no recording window to keep open.

  1. 01

    Keep the buffer

    The Vice service starts at login and keeps a rolling buffer while you play.

  2. 02

    Save a Clip or start a Session

    Press F9 for the last 20 seconds. Double-tap to record a full Session.

  3. 03

    Use the result

    Trim or edit it, move it to a Playlist, or share it with a public link.

What Vice includes

The useful parts are built in.

Vice covers capture, editing, sharing, and organization without an account or subscription.

Clip sharing

Share Clips without uploading them.

Vice creates a free public link directly from your machine. There is no Vice file-size limit or upload queue, and the link plays as an embed in Discord.

No upload Copy the link and send it.

Capture performance

Capture with negligible overhead.

Vice uses gpu-screen-recorder to capture through NVENC or VAAPI on NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel instead of continuously compositing a scene.

<1% Typical CPU use.

Trimmer and editor

Finish the Clip inside Vice.

Trim a Clip without re-encoding it, or use the multi-track editor for video, audio, text, and transitions.

Built in No separate editor required.

Game context

Let Vice handle the organization.

Vice detects known games, tags Clip filenames, files them into matching Playlists, and shows the active game through Discord Rich Presence.

Automatic Tags, Playlists, and presence.

Ownership

Use it without an account.

Vice is free, open source, and licensed under GPL-3.0. It has no subscription and no telemetry.
