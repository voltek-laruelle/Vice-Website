How it works

Vice runs in the background until you need it.

  1. 01

    Keep the buffer

    Vice is always ready while your game is running.

  2. 02

    Save a Clip or start a Session

    Press F9 for the last 20 seconds. Double-tap to record a full Session.

  3. 03

    Trim, edit, or share

    Use the built-in tools, file it into a Playlist, or copy its public link.

Included with Vice

Capture, editing, sharing, and organization are built in.

There is no account, subscription, upload queue, or separate editor to manage.

F9

Clips and Sessions

Save the replay or record the full match.

A silent daemon keeps the rolling buffer ready at login. Press once for a Clip, or double-tap to start a Session and add highlights while you play.

Typical CPU use

Driver-level capture.

Vice captures through NVENC or VAAPI on NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel, with typical CPU use under one percent.

Free, unlimited sharing

Share Clips by link.

There is no Vice file-size limit or upload step. Paste the public link into Discord and the Clip plays as an embed.
